SOODAL LIFE V159 - Expert offer and group recruitment marketplace

Release scope
- Existing advertising remains unchanged and a separate expert offer/group recruitment module is added.
- Only active catalog services approved for the expert can be selected.
- Nationwide recruitment requires the service's nationwide/remote approval; local recruitment accepts at most five active districts.
- Capacity x KRW 5,000 is reserved. Only confirmed participants are captured, and unused or failed recruitment funds are released/restored.
- Customer home feed, public list/detail, one-click neighborhood and service interests are included.
- Targeting uses direct interests, request/transaction history, saved district information and 30-day service detail signals; exact addresses are never exposed to experts.
- Nationwide notifications use WEB/PUSH; local notifications use WEB/PUSH/SMS. Kakao AlimTalk is excluded.
- Marketing legal consent and per-channel consent are required for active delivery.
- SMS/PUSH external delivery is fail-closed until ProviderProposals:MarketingTermsFinalized=true.
- PUSH remains blocked until ProviderProposals:PwaPushEnabled=true and the channel is enabled.
